What you are looking at

Every position is a drilled assay interval, placed by downhole survey. Colour is grade on a log scale, pale to dark, one metal at a time. Grades run from under a thousandth of a percent to nearly fifty, so a linear scale would put a barren sample and an ore sample in the same shade.

The ground above them is the surveyed terrain mesh. It opens as a wireframe because a solid surface hides everything beneath it.

Switch to Predictive and the samples recolour. The pink prism is a block of ground set aside before any modelling began. The estimate was built from the grey positions only and never saw an assay inside the pink.

Click the prism, or the button above, to go inside it and see how close the estimate came.

Resistivity draws the inverted results of the dipole-dipole IP and resistivity survey as vertical sections beneath each survey line. One property is shown at a time. Darker means more anomalous: higher chargeability, or lower resistivity. Only the parts of the survey that fall inside this scene are drawn.
