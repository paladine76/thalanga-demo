What you are looking at

Every position here was withheld. The estimate was built from the drilling outside this block and never saw a single assay inside it, so the measured view is the answer sheet.

Measured is what the core actually assayed. Kriged is ordinary kriging, which estimates the most likely grade at each position. Simulated is sequential Gaussian simulation, which draws grades that reproduce the variability of the deposit rather than its average.

Raise the cutoff and watch the kriged view empty first. Kriging minimises error position by position, and the price is a collapsed grade distribution: it reports no ground at all above the grades that carry a mine.

A single realisation is noisy where kriging is smooth. That is the design, not a fault. Kriging answers what grade sits here; simulation answers how much ground lies above a cutoff, and how sure we are.
